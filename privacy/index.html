Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how Tin Tai Chinh 60s Uploader ("the Service"), an internal tool of Tin Tài Chính 60 Giây, handles data when connecting to TikTok.

1. Data we access

  • Basic account info (user.info.basic): display name, avatar and open ID of the TikTok account our team connects, used only to confirm the correct channel.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok after authorization, used only to upload our videos.
  • Videos and captions that our team produces and uploads.

2. How we use it

We use this data solely to upload our own videos to our own TikTok account as drafts. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and account info are stored only on our team's own computer and are never published. We do not run a public database of user data.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok through its official APIs.

5. Other TikTok users

The Service does not access or collect data about other TikTok users, followers or viewers.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep tokens only while the Service is in use. You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, after which we delete the stored tokens.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Contact us via TikTok @tintaichinh60s or YouTube @tintaichinh60s.
