Tin Tai Chinh 60s Uploader

Tin Tài Chính 60 Giây is a Vietnamese finance news channel that summarises the day's market, economy and policy news in 60-second vertical videos.

Tin Tai Chinh 60s Uploader is an internal tool used only by our team. It uploads videos we produce ourselves to our own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The operator connects our TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit and approves access.
  2. The tool shows the connected account's name and avatar to confirm the correct channel.
  3. Each finished video is uploaded to the TikTok inbox as a draft using the Content Posting API.
  4. The creator reviews the draft in the TikTok app, edits the caption, chooses privacy settings and publishes manually.
